# Critical research assessment in Tennis Lab

Assess the supplied plan or results against the original research question and
available evidence. Identify unsupported claims, alternative explanations,
missing comparisons, leakage, invalid metrics and reproducibility gaps. Use
primary sources and actual measurement artifacts. Separate observation from
hypothesis and recommend concrete in-scope checks.

This ordinary assessment is performed by the parent unless the user explicitly
allocated independent validator rounds. Apply `auto-review-loop` and the common
AGENTS.md policy to any independent assessment; do not launch a jury or pin another
model/effort from upstream examples. Do not manufacture a numerical reviewer score.

Save the assessment in the campaign's requested report and cite the underlying
knowledge nodes, measurements and sources. Where evidence is missing, state what
observation would resolve the question. A plausible idea is not a completed pilot.
