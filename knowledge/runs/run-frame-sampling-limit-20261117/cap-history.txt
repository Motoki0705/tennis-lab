Extracted from PR #915 (9b8f7d3c, 382005b4, 4211b108, 2b084442 and the
pipeline parts of 5d26aa0d/09b3c76b/04deb002). The fixed-track PLCS Re-ID

- player_association / court_side nodes are defined by tennis_scene-owned
  artifact contracts (components/identity.py, schema version 2) and are
  load-only until #933 / #932 add implementations; requesting execution
  fails when the definition is built.

  frame_sampling.max_frames (pipeline/frame_sampling.py replaces
  utilts/timeline.py).
- The historical manual association decoder moves to
  src/tasks/plcs/data/manual_association.py, removing the tasks ->
  tennis_scene reverse dependency of the residual real-clip loader.
- The colab job protects people_models.yolo_checkpoint instead of the
  removed Re-ID/side checkpoints.
